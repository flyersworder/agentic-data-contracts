Retrieval-augmented ``context layers'' --- curated domain documentation an
analytics agent fetches at query time --- are reported to produce large
accuracy gains on text-to-SQL benchmarks. Those results compare a system with
a layer against one without, and two accuracy numbers cannot say which part
of the layer did the work: the semantic content, the retrieval scaffolding
that delivers it, or the pre-computed views that usually accompany it.

We report a controlled four-arm ablation on DABStep across three model
families. The fourth arm, \armH{}, is derived mechanically from the frozen
contract: every field of prose is emptied while the tool surface, retrieval
instruction, table allow-list and operation rules stay byte-for-byte fixed.

Contract-delivered semantics raise hard-task accuracy from 13.9\% to 55.1\%,
22.6\% to 56.6\% and 37.0\% to 77.4\%, beating the same knowledge pasted
verbatim into the prompt on all three models ($p{\le}0.0067$, paired
McNemar). The content step dominates the scaffolding step everywhere, but the
scaffolding step is not zero: undetectable on two models and worth $+14.2$
points ($p{=}4{\times}10^{-7}$) on the third. \textbf{A null across two
adjacent models is weak evidence of absence.}

The contract is also executable. Transcribing its declared SQL expressions
into two views reproduces gold on all 176 tasks it covers, 53\% of the hard
split, so each arm's accuracy can be read as efficiency against a proven
ceiling. The contract arm recovers 60.8\%, 62.7\% and \textbf{94.9\%} of what
it was given, against 45.9\%, 46.5\% and 56.1\% on tasks that compose the same
semantics further. \textbf{Capability arrives unevenly}: the cost of making an
agent derive a query rather than pre-computing it is transient, as we
predicted publicly before the third run; the cost of the reasoning built above
that query is not.

The advantage over a hand-written prompt is not monotone in capability, and
the effect is not detectable outside the domain the contract describes. One
run lost 29\% of its rows to rate-limiting and is underpowered throughout. The
harness, the frozen artifact, all 4{,}812 transcripts and raw results are
released.
