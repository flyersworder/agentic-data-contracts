\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Context layers work; the open question was which part of one does the
work. On two benchmarks and seven models the answer is the knowledge:
emptying a contract removes most of its gain, and what looked like an
advantage of delivery was mostly two facts the manual left out. A contract
pays where it states something true that the documentation leaves out;
its author can supply what the documentation or the data determines, and
a convention only when the gold shares the author's default.

Delivery still matters, for other reasons. On the GPT models, looking a
contract up is cheaper from twice a database's size, and it runs where the
prompt no longer fits; its log traces a wrong answer to the entry behind
it. No run whose prompt stated the forbidden operations, in the tools or
as text, corrupted its database copy, against 50 runs without them,
nearly all on one model.

\ifextended
\paragraph{Future work.} The knowledge ladder run together on two models,
with a retrieval arm over the original documentation; human raters on a
sample of the determinacy labels; a second benchmark whose documentation
states rules once for many tasks; a scale test with paraphrased questions
and padding from one warehouse; and a validator that can state ``a filter
on this list column must accept the empty-list wildcard''.

\section*{Artifact Availability}

The library, the harness, every contract and compiler, the analysis
scripts, the blind ratings, the run commits and the raw result rows are
tracked at \url{https://github.com/flyersworder/agentic-data-contracts}.

model, endpoint, token counts, cost and a post-run integrity check of its
database copy. The transcripts of every DABStep and constructed-question
run are attached to the releases tagged \texttt{dabstep-eval-2026-09}
and \texttt{dabstep-eval-2026-10}, with SHA-256 digests. LiveSQLBench's
private gold, test cases and task mapping are not redistributed, nor are
its transcripts, since one graded correct is a solution the benchmark
keeps private; its rows carry a hash of the gold result, and the analyses
that use the gold print aggregates only.
