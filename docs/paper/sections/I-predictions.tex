The table holds 76 predictions in 18 commits, 53 before experiment~5d, 4
for it, 6 for 5e and 13 for experiment~7: 36 held (25 directional, 7
registered ties, which hold by not rejecting, and 4 one-sided bounds,
which hold by an interval), 3 held in part (``mostly holds'' is counted
here), 28 failed, 8 were not read$^d$,
and one was read as equivalent, which its commit registered as a separate
outcome. Rows listing several numbers count each. LiveSQLBench's (6) was fixed after \mQwen{}'s per-arm totals for two
repeats were seen, before the statistic it predicts. Experiment 4b's
predictions were first written in its design (\texttt{d76d21e}) and
re-specified in \texttt{a7ee6b4}, before any contract was authored. Grades
are as made at the time; notes $a$--$c$ are from review: $^a$the compiled
contract's 45.6\% and the manual's 45.3\% come from different runs, so
``between'' is not established; $^b$the test is over questions, which come
from five gaps, and over gaps it is four to none with one tie, one-sided
$p{=}0.06$; $^c$(32) holds with 12 of 15 gap-draws, crediting three
wildcard draws that never compute specificity; scored partial, recovery is
9 of 15 and (32) fails. $^d$Fixed before the run: when the manipulation
check (44) fails, (45)--(48) are reported (\appref{app:dab}) and not read as
support.
