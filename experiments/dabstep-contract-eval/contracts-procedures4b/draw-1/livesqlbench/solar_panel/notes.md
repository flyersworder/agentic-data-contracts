Potential Snapshot Yield uses energy_yield.availability_pct directly as a fraction, not the documentation’s 0–100 percentage divided by 100: it is stored as 0.95–0.9999 in all 998 rows. efficiency.soil_loss_pct is a true percentage (observed 0.02–14.99). Recoverable Energy Loss, the Technology Monthly Energy Loss Ratio, its band, and Technology Recoverable Energy consequently use potential yields calculated with that fraction.

In warranty_settle_rules, no criterion is NULL: “no restriction” is stored as the literal 'ALL' in all six criterion columns. Rule matching treats both 'ALL' and NULL as wildcards, and neither counts toward specificity. The contract reports that 926 of 998 snapshots currently match a rule.

The contract reports the current full-fleet warranty settlement total as 2,142,835.06 USD. Its settlement amount calculation also substitutes the standard 300.00 USD fixed fee or 5.00% coverage individually when the respective selected-rule value is NULL, in addition to using those terms when there is no governing rule.

Performance Ratio uses the stored energy_yield.specific_yield_kwh_kw divided by irradiance.poa_irr_w_m2 converted from W/m² to kW/m². Only instantaneous POA irradiance is stored, not the documentation’s total in-plane irradiation in kWh/m², so the resulting metric is not on the 0–1 scale. Underperforming Asset instead tests the separately stored energy_yield.performance_ratio against 0.75; that field ranges from 0.7 to 0.9 and is NULL in 400 rows, which are not flagged.

The inspection table has only four rows, keyed by inspection method, with no key to snapshots or plants. Neglected Asset therefore requires a snapshot’s Critical Alert plus the fleet-wide existence of one inspection row satisfying both Overdue Critical Maintenance and Data Quality Concern; the contract says 'IR Thermal', with a data quality score of 19.9, currently satisfies the inspection part. The conditions must occur on the same inspection row, not necessarily on an inspection linked to the asset.

For Annual Degradation Rate, Age in Years is the number of days from plants.goliveon to CURRENT_DATE divided by 365.25, rather than age at the snapshot. The contract says 445 of 998 snapshot timestamps precede their plant’s go-live date. It reads cumulative degradation from efficiency.cumulative_deg_pct and reports ADR in percent per year. Degradation-Adjusted Capacity uses that ADR divided by 100 as a fraction and reports capacity in MW from plants.cap_mw.

Mean Repair Cost takes “total time” to mean hours from plants.goliveon to CURRENT_DATE, using operational_metrics.maintcost and mtbfh for the snapshot. The documentation does not specify this time window.

Power Capacity Factor returns the stored energy_yield.capacity_factor_pct, already a percentage (observed 15.01–29.99), rather than calculating from energy and capacity: the period’s hours are not stored and must be supplied to calculate the documented formula.

Specific Yield is calculated per snapshot from energy_yield.energy_yield_kwh and the snapshot plant’s plants.cap_mw converted from MW to kW. The contract also identifies a separate stored energy_yield.specific_yield_kwh_kw value; its Performance Ratio uses that stored value rather than the calculated Specific Yield metric.

Thermal Loss Factor uses environmental_conditions.env_snapshot temperatures.cell_temp_c, 25°C as the reference, and the plant’s panel_models.tempcoeff via plants.modhook. The coefficient is stored in %/°C (for example, -0.389), so the result is in percentage points; cell_temp_c is NULL in 398 rows and tempcoeff in 237 models. Temperature-Corrected Performance takes actual power as electrical_performance.elec_perf_snapshot power.power_now_w in watts (NULL in 400 rows) and converts the thermal loss from percentage points to a fraction before adjusting it.

System Power Loss Ratio reads power.power_loss_w and power.power_now_w from the electrical snapshot; both are NULL in 400 rows. Effective Power Output interprets gross power as the reported power.power_now_w in watts, rather than a separate gross-power field.

For System Unavailability and System Availability, MTTR and MTBF come from operational_metrics.mttrh and mtbfh, in hours; mtbfh is NULL in 400 of 998 rows, making those metrics NULL there. The JSON energy_yield.availability_pct is a separate stored fraction, not the MTTR/MTBF formula. Chronic Downtime Asset uses formula-derived availability below 0.95, not the JSON availability value.

Operational Expenditure Index uses operational_metrics.maintcost, cleancost and replcost divided by plants.cap_mw, producing cost per MW. No numeric High-Cost Asset threshold or flag metric is defined: the asker must supply the threshold. Decommissioning Candidate likewise requires a supplied High-Cost threshold, to combine with Neglected Asset.

Maintenance Cost to Revenue Impact Ratio reads operational_metrics.revloss, stored as text such as '$13,375.59 ', stripping the dollar sign, commas and surrounding spaces before converting it to a number. Its metric divides one snapshot’s maintcost by that parsed revenue loss; for totals, the contract says to divide the sum of maintenance costs by the sum of revenue losses.

High Degradation Panel Model uses panel_models.degyrrate, stored in percent per year (0.3–1.2), and flags values strictly greater than 0.7%. Accelerated Aging Asset has no defined metric: the documentation gives no numeric “high” ADR threshold, so the threshold must be stated and annual_degradation_rate combined with is_major_module_degradation.

The contract fixes example thresholds as metric definitions: Severe Soiling Condition is soil_loss_pct > 15% (no current row exceeds it); Data Quality Concern is inspection.dqscore < 50; Warranty Claim Risk is plants.warrstate = 'Claimed' with plants.warrclaims > 2; and Underperforming Asset and Chronic Downtime Asset use the stored-PR and formula-availability thresholds described above. The data quality metric has one row per inspection method, while the warranty-claim metric has one row per plant.

The contract locates other condition inputs: tracker state, mounting structure status, busbar corrosion, delamination and microcrack count, grounding status and bypass diode status are in mechanical_condition.mech_health_snapshot; High Maintenance and Replacement Priority use alert.maintprio and alert.replprio; environmental risk uses plants.envtag. Critical Alert requires both priority fields to be 'High', not alert.alrtstate = 'Critical'. Electrical Integrity Failure treats any non-'Normal' bypass_diode_status as a failure (the contract identifies 'Failed' and 'Partial'). Major Module Degradation requires 'Severe' busbar corrosion, 'Major' delamination and more than five microcracks together.

The contract locates the electrical and environmental measurements in JSON groups of electrical_performance.elec_perf_snapshot and environmental_conditions.env_snapshot. Its per-snapshot metrics use the fields energy_yield.energy_yield_kwh, efficiency.soil_loss_pct, inverter.inverter_eff_pct, iv_curve.fill_factor_initial and fill_factor_now, vmp_initial_v and vmp_now_v, and imp_initial_a and imp_now_a. Current Degradation Factor is NULL where imp_now_a is NULL; IV curve and power denominators of zero produce NULL rather than a quotient.

Some JSON numbers are stored as integers: the contract reads JSON numbers as text and casts them to double precision before arithmetic. Other fields require parsing units or formatting: iv_curve.series_res_ohm is text like '0.17 Ω', efficiency.spectral_mismatch like '100.50%' (factor 1.005), panel_models.nomoptempc like '45.7 °C', alert.alrtcnt like '6 events', and operational_metrics.revloss like '$13,375.59 '. panel_models.tempcoeff is NULL in 237 models; power.power_now_w, power.power_loss_w and stored performance_ratio are NULL in 400 snapshots.
